Taking care of the military community for over 100 years

USAA was founded in 1922 when a group of soldiers insured each other because other companies considered their military lifestyle to be too risky.

Today, we're still serving the military community and their families with award-winning service and support. Because for us, membership has always been about taking care of our own.

Become a member Learn more about our mission

Who can join USAA

If you're military or their eligible family, you're part of our community and could join USAA today. Once you join, you can share membership with your spouse, kids and then grandkids.

 

We're ready to serve you if you're a:

  • Current military member.
  • Veteran who honorably served.
  • National Guard or Reservist.
  • Contracted cadet or midshipman.
  • Eligible officer candidate or warrant officer candidate.
  • Spouse or kid of a member.

    Credit Card FAQ

    If you're new to using a credit card, here's some general information:

    • You'll need to apply and be approved for a credit card. Be sure you review a credit card's rates and fees before you apply.
    • You'll have a credit limit for your card, which is the maximum amount you can charge in transactions, such as purchases and payments. If you go over your credit limit, you could be charged a fee.
    • When you use credit cards to make purchases or payments, you'll see the transactions listed on your monthly statement. If you don't pay your statement balance in full by the due date, interest will be charged on your remaining balance.
    • Review all the features of the credit cards you're considering.
    • Think about credit cards that offer rewards, lower interest rates and other benefits like travel insurance.
    • Look at the annual percentage rate (APR) and fees for transactions like balance transfers and cash advances. USAA credit cards have the same APR whether you're transferring a balance, getting a cash advance or making a purchase.
    • Check for an annual fee. This is an amount some cards ask you to pay every year to have the card.
    Yes, you can. The number of credit cards you have is a personal choice based on your needs and ability to manage your credit responsibly.
    If you're a USAA member, you can apply for any of the credit cards we offer. Our products and services are open to current and former military and their spouses. Children of USAA members can join, too. To see if you qualify for USAA membership, view our eligibility guidelines.
    • We offer secure logons for your credit card account, whether you're on the USAA Mobile App or usaa.com.
    • You'll have zero liability for unauthorized transactions to your credit card.see note 17
    • You can set up alerts for your account.
    • You can block or unblock your credit card online or by phone.
    • If your card is lost or stolen, you can report it online or by phone.

    USAA offers Credit Cards with cash back and point reward programs.

    Credit cards with a cash back reward program, cash back is earned as a percentage of the Qualifying Purchase amount. For example, if your card offers 1% cash back on a $150 purchase, you could earn $1.50 in cash back rewards.

    Credit cards with a point reward program, the number of reward points earned is based on the dollar amount spent on each Qualifying Purchase. For example, if you earn 2X points on gas and spend $50 filling up, you could earn 100 reward points.

    Cash back for Eagle Ascend™ and Eagle Adapt™ is earned in the form of points. Each $1.00 spent on Qualifying Purchases is equal to 1 point.

    Cash back and points earned with USAA Accounts can be redeemed for various rewards, including airline travel or merchandise. The value of each point may vary depending on redemption type and, in some instances, may also vary depending on the USAA Accounts you have that earn points.see note 1​
